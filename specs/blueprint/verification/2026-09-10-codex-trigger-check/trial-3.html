Ledgerly system architecture

Synchronous request paths, asynchronous order events, and storage ownership. Service boundaries and edge labels reflect the contracts stated in AGENTS.md.

directed interaction or ownership system or external boundary

1. Service topology and synchronous requests

web reaches backend capabilities only through gateway; billing and notification providers remain behind their owning adapters.

2. Event flows over bus

orders publishes lifecycle events; billing consumes placement only, while notifier consumes placement and fulfillment.

3. Storage ownership

Each owning service is the only reader/writer of its Postgres schema. catalog separately owns its read cache; notifier has no database.
